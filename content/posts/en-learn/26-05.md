### Elvis operator (?:)

The Elvis operator (written as ?:) is a binary operator used in programming to provide a default fallback value when a primary expression evaluates to null, undefined, or false. It gets its name because the symbol, when viewed sideways, resembles Elvis Presley’s signature hairstyle.

> sideways: with one side facing forwards 侧着；侧面朝前
> resemble: to be like or similar to

> Elvis Aaron Presley, known as the "King of Rock and Roll", was an American singer and actor who revolutionized popular music by blending blues, country, and gospel. His stage presence and near-perfect pitch made him a cultural icon of the 20th century. Presley's impact extended beyond music, as he's credited with helping to end segregation in the US. He sold an estimated one billion records worldwide, more than any other artist in history. 

> segregation: the act or policy of separating people of different races, religions or sexes and treating them in a different way（对不同种族、宗教或性别的人所采取的）隔离并区别对待，隔离政策


### metaphor, elevation, tonal

Material surface is the central metaphor in material design. Each surface exists at a given elevation, which influences how that piece of surface visually relates to other surfaces and how that surface is modified by tonal variance.

metaphor: /ˈmetəfɔːr/ 隐喻;暗喻;比喻的说法 A metaphor is an imaginative way of describing something by referring to something else which is the same in a particular way. For example, if you want to say that someone is very shy and frightened of things, you might say that they are a mouse.

elevation: /ˌelɪˈveɪʃn/ 海拔 The elevation of a place is its height above sea level.

tonal: /ˈtəʊnl/ 音调的;声调的;调性的 Tonal means relating to the qualities or pitch of a sound or to the tonality of a piece of music. 这里指色调 relating to tones of sound or color 音调的；声调的；色调的

## Phrases

### lay the groundwork

Dependency injection (DI) is a technique widely used in programming and well suited to Android development. By following the principles of DI, you lay the groundwork for good app architecture.

是“打地基”（groundwork 指地基、基础工作），比喻“为……奠定基础”或“做好前期准备工作”。

The preliminary meetings laid the groundwork for the merger.
（前期的会议为这次合并奠定了基础。）

We need to lay the groundwork before we launch the new product.
（在发布新产品之前，我们需要做好前期准备工作。）

His early research laid the groundwork for modern genetics.
（他的早期研究为现代遗传学奠定了基础。）

The diplomats are trying to lay the groundwork for peace talks.
（外交官们正试图为和平谈判做前期准备。）

近义表达

- Pave the way (for)：为……铺平道路
- Set the stage (for)：为……做好准备（搭好舞台）
- Make preparations (for)：做准备
- Provide a foundation (for)：提供基础
